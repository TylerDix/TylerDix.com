Landed

The lowest total to your door for a fragrance, from US stores that showed it in stock today, and plainly who each store is.

How it works

Landed checks prices at US fragrance stores through the day. For each bottle it ranks only the stores that showed it in stock in the last 24 hours, by the price plus shipping. Every price also shows its cost per ml, so different bottle sizes compare fairly.

Each listing says what kind of store it is (the brand's own store, a specialty boutique, or a grey-market discounter), where it's based, and its return policy, taken from the store's own site. Price history shows the lowest total per day.

Stores and commissions

Landed may earn a commission when you buy through a link. Commission never affects ranking. Stores are always ordered by total to your door.

Privacy

The app has no accounts and collects no personal data. Your watchlist stays on your phone. When you open a store, you're on that store's website, under its privacy policy.

Landed is an independent app by Tyler Dix. It isn't affiliated with any fragrance house or store.
